# Southbay Motor Credit: Prime Vehicle Loan Policy SB-AUTO-PRIME, version 2026.1 (SYNTHETIC)

Effective from 1 January 2026. Product: new-car-fixed. Currency: INR. Jurisdiction: India.

## Page 1

### Part A. Scope

A1 Scope. Synthetic prime lending policy for new private passenger vehicles in India, denominated in INR. For capstone demonstration only.

### Part B. Applicant eligibility

B1 Credit score. A CIBIL credit score of at least 700 is required. Other bureaus are not accepted.

B2 Income. Qualifying monthly income must be at least INR 40,000.

B3 Employment. Salaried applicants need 12 months of continuous employment; self-employed applicants need 36 months in business. Other categories are ineligible.

B4 Active loans. No more than 2 active loans are permitted at the time of application.

## Page 2

### Part C. Loan terms

C1 Debt-service limit. All monthly debt obligations including the new EMI must not exceed 40% of qualifying monthly income.

C2 Rates. Fixed nominal annual rate compounded monthly: score 750 to 900 at 9.25%; score 700 to 749 at 10.5%.

C3 Tenure. Tenure must be between 12 and 60 months.

C4 Loan amount. Principal must be between INR 2,00,000 and INR 30,00,000.

C5 Loan-to-value. Maximum loan-to-value is 80% of the on-road price.

C7 Credit risk. Applications meeting parts B and C are approved only if the validated credit-risk model (vla-eligibility) estimates an approval probability of at least 0.88.

## Page 3

C6 Fees. Processing fee is 1.5% of principal, minimum INR 5,000, maximum INR 25,000, payable upfront.

### Part D. Vehicles and calculations

D1 Vehicles. Commercial vehicles are excluded. Prices older than 90 days must be re-verified.

D2 Rounding. EMI rounded half-up to 0.01; principal rounded down to INR 1,000.

### Part E. Exceptions and documentation

E1 Exceptions. Exceptions to parts B and C are not permitted for the prime product. Declined applicants may be offered the standard product of another lender.

E2 Documentation. Identity and address proof, six months of bank statements, salary slips or two years of tax returns, and the dealer proforma invoice.
