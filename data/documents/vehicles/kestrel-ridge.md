# Kestrel Ridge brochure (synthetic)

Model key: kestrel-ridge. Body type: suv. Use: private.

## Overview

The Ridge is a sub-four-metre SUV with a turbo-petrol option and a five-star synthetic crash rating, popular with young families upgrading from a hatchback.

## Variants and prices

- E (vehicle id kes-ridge-e): petrol, manual, 5 seats, 1199 cc, 65 kW, efficiency 17.4 km/l, 6 airbags. Ex-showroom price in Maharashtra INR 8,90,000.
- S (vehicle id kes-ridge-s): petrol, manual, 5 seats, 1199 cc, 65 kW, efficiency 17.4 km/l, 6 airbags. Ex-showroom price in Maharashtra INR 10,20,000. Adds: Electric sunroof; Auto headlamps.
- S Turbo DCT (vehicle id kes-ridge-s-turbo-at): petrol, automatic, 5 seats, 1199 cc, 88 kW, efficiency 16.2 km/l, 6 airbags. Ex-showroom price in Maharashtra INR 12,10,000. Adds: Paddle shifters; Ventilated seats.

## Key features

- 7-inch touchscreen
