# Kestrel Trail brochure (synthetic)

Model key: kestrel-trail. Body type: suv. Use: private.

## Overview

The Trail is a body-on-frame SUV with strong low-end torque and generous ground clearance for rough roads; the X 4x4 adds a third row and low-range transfer case.

## Variants and prices

- S (vehicle id kes-trail-s): diesel, manual, 5 seats, 1493 cc, 85 kW, efficiency 19.1 km/l, 6 airbags. Ex-showroom price in Maharashtra INR 16,80,000.
- X 4x4 (vehicle id kes-trail-x-4x4): diesel, automatic, 7 seats, 2184 cc, 128 kW, efficiency 14.8 km/l, 6 airbags. Ex-showroom price in Maharashtra INR 24,50,000. Adds: Low-range 4x4 transfer case; Third-row seats.
